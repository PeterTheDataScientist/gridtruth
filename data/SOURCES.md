## What is deliberately not used

**Third-party load shedding apps and their archives.** Several commercial services resell utility schedule data under their own terms. Their data is not used as a label source or otherwise, anywhere in this project. Where labelled outage data is needed for calibration, it comes from the utility's own published record.

**Anything behind a login or paywall.** Every source above is publicly reachable without credentials, except NASA Earthdata, which is free to register for and whose terms permit this use.

## Requests

Fetches identify themselves with a descriptive user agent and a link to this repository, run on a schedule measured in hours rather than seconds, and cache by content hash so an unchanged page is not re-downloaded. If you operate one of these sites and want the schedule changed or collection stopped, open an issue and it will be honoured.
